# Ethical Use Policy

This repository is licensed under Apache-2.0. In addition to legal license terms, this policy defines intended and prohibited uses for organizations adopting this project.

## Intended use

- Data pipeline development for lawful, privacy-respecting, and non-discriminatory operations.
- Internal analytics, ETL, and data quality workflows with proper governance.

## Prohibited use

Do not use this project to build, operate, or support systems that:

- Perform unlawful surveillance or unauthorized data collection.
- Target individuals or groups for discrimination, harassment, or exclusion.
- Process sensitive personal data without lawful basis, notice, and controls.
- Enable human rights abuses, violence, or suppression of civil liberties.
- Intentionally evade regulatory, compliance, or contractual obligations.

## Organizational requirements

Organizations using this project should:

- Conduct a privacy and security review before production deployment.
- Apply data minimization and retention controls.
- Restrict access by least privilege.
- Maintain audit trails for pipeline runs and incidents.
- Provide internal escalation and incident response mechanisms.

## Enforcement posture

This policy is normative guidance for responsible adoption and governance. For binding obligations in commercial settings, include equivalent terms in contracts, platform terms, or internal policy controls.
